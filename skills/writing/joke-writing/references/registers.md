# Humor registers (the tone / the flavor)

> **Form != register.** The *form* is the wiring (one-liner, observational, headline, roast, cringe...). The *register* is the flavor, the target, the tone. Any one form works in any register. This table says, for each requested register, which **engine** to fire up, which **dial** to set, which **go-to form** to reach for, the **trap**, and an **example**.

Every register reduces to the 5 levers in `comedy-kb.md` (incongruity, benign distance, timing, commitment/POV, stakes) plus the secondary engines (relief = taboo; superiority = mockery). None is bolt-on: each is a *preset* of the same machine.

Cross-cutting golden rule: **the register changes the content and the target, never the structural requirement.** A dark, crude, or political joke must ALWAYS pass the 3 gates (real violation, benign, pivot last). The register is not an excuse to skip the mechanics.

---

## Dark
- **Engine:** severe violation (death, illness, disaster) + relief (discharge of the taboo).
- **Dial:** distance **high** (Lever 2, inverted-U: the graver the violation, the more *distance* makes it funny). Set it via: past/hypothetical, third person, the absurd, the grotesque silver-lining. Start from the real violation and dial safety *inward*, never the reverse.
- **Form:** one-liner, headline, deadpan, definition.
- **Target/ethics:** the cosmic, the powerful, the self - **not** a fresh, identifiable victim ("too soon" kills).
- **Trap:** confusing shock with funny. Something merely horrible with no resolution isn't a joke, it's a threat. You need the turn.
- **Example:** *"The upside of climate collapse: we'll finally stop getting reminders about our dental appointments."*

## Brit (British)
- **Engine:** understatement + self-deprecation + class/status awareness + social embarrassment. (A sensibility cluster, not a single technique.)
- **Dial:** **downplay** everything. Never flag the joke (absolute deadpan). Pessimism carried lightly. Leave the implication to the reader.
- **Form:** character/cringe (The Office), observational, dry one-liner.
- **Trap:** underplaying an already-minor event ("who cares"). Understatement is only funny about something big.
- **Example:** *"It was a lovely wedding. Very few people cried, and most of them for the right reasons."*

## Crude / filthy
- **Engine:** relief - discharge of the taboo (body, sex, scatology). The play-frame keeps it benign.
- **Dial:** the taboo is the **content**, never a substitute for structure. The pivot/surprise stays mandatory.
- **Form:** act-out, exaggeration, the "shock" filter (Dikkers).
- **Trap:** crude with no incongruity = just dirty. The vulgarity must *serve* a punch, not replace it. Precise-crude beats vague-crude.
- **Example (the register, not the worst of it):** *"My doctor told me to keep an eye on my body. He didn't mention he'd been doing it with binoculars from the parking lot."* (The voyeurism taboo serves a turn, it doesn't replace it.)

## Cringe (awkward)
- **Engine:** empathy + *reduced* distance (the opposite of dark). Commitment that spirals.
- **Dial:** **close in**. Small social misstep -> the character refuses to back down -> each line escalates the absurd cover-up. He **never** apologizes.
- **Form:** character (already a form in its own right in the KB - see S2).
- **Trap:** making the character self-aware/sorry kills the discomfort. The comedy is the gap between his insistence and the visible collapse.
- **Example:** the guy who has obviously crashed the hot-dog car into the storefront and shouts along with the crowd *"we're all trying to find the guy who did this!"* while quietly looting.

## Situational / sniper
- **Engine:** specificity + callback + reading the context (KB S7). The joke *is born from* and stays glued to the exact situation.
- **Dial:** localize as hard as possible. One word, ultra-precise, that hits the target the whole room already has in mind. The "sniper" = one line landing on a detail shared at that exact moment.
- **Form:** one-liner, QRT, closing callback.
- **Trap:** generic = a stray bullet. If it would work in any context, it isn't sniper work.
- **Example:** (contextual by nature) take a word the target dropped 3 minutes earlier and flip it back right when they think they're in the clear.

## Cutting (biting)
- **Engine:** superiority + the third thought + "saying the thing everyone leaves unsaid."
- **Dial:** maximum economy, the sharp truth placed as the **last word**. No fat. The precision *is* the blade.
- **Form:** definition, observational with an edge, "quiet-part" headline.
- **Trap:** mean with no truth underneath = just bitterness. The bite has to reveal something true, not just wound.
- **Example:** *"Networking: standing in a room telling people what you do until one of them needs it."*

## Political / satirical
- **Engine:** superiority + false analogy (one-word swap) / saying-the-quiet-part + exaggerating a real position to the point of absurdity.
- **Dial:** **punch up** (the default ethics setting: power, not the vulnerable) + **freshness contract** (name the topic's 3-5 clichéd takes, then ban them). An owned POV is mandatory.
- **Form:** headline (Onion), definition, exaggeration.
- **Trap:** the "both-sides" / consensus take = death (anti-pattern 9). This is EXACTLY where AI's blandness is worst. Pick an angle that splits a room.
- **Example:** *"New austerity plan unveiled: everyone tightens their belt, except the people who make the belts."*

## Mocking / roast
- **Engine:** direct superiority (give the audience someone to feel superior to).
- **Dial:** the roast engine - list the traits, mark the unflattering ones, **collide two traits** or exaggerate one to an absurd-but-recognizable extreme. **Imply, never state** the insult.
- **Form:** roast, one-liner.
- **Target/ethics:** power, the self, or a consenting target. Not a vulnerable third party.
- **Example:** *"He's so centered and present he's never once been present on time."*

## Absurd / surreal
- **Engine:** pure incongruity, *internal logic* maintained, weak resolution.
- **Dial:** this is NOT random. Keep an internal rule the mind can grab onto (Steven Wright literalism, Demetri Martin inversions). The calm inside the absurd logic = the funny.
- **Form:** absurdist, literal one-liner.
- **Trap:** dropping all logic = it stops tracking, it becomes noise.
- **Example:** *"I have a map of the United States. Actual size."*

## Deadpan / dry
- This is a **delivery**, applicable to almost every register above, not a content type. State the absurd/dark/cutting thing flatly, confidently, no wink, nothing after it. The flatness and the missing wink *are* the delivery. The smallest break in commitment kills it.

## Post-ironic / internet (meta-irony, new sincerity)
- **Engine:** the ironic wrapper serves as cover to say something true/vulnerable (KB S2).
- **Dial:** signal incoming mockery, then deliver the sincere thing. Meta-irony = stack the frames until sincere/mocking blur.
- **Trap (the dark mirror):** the same ambiguity gives cover to disguised bigotry ("it's just a joke" as an alibi). If a line only works as an attack on a vulnerable group, it isn't post-irony, it's the thing it pretends to mock. Don't write it.

---

## How to combine (register x form x target)
1. **Register** = flavor/dial (this table). 2. **Form** = wiring (KB S2). 3. **POV/attitude** = who's speaking (SKILL S0). The three are orthogonal: "dark humor" (register) in a "headline" (form) with an "enthusiastic bureaucrat" POV gives a macabre Onion headline. "Brit" + "definition" + a "resigned" POV gives a dry, jaded definition.

If the user specifies no register: pick the one the **topic** calls for (a power topic -> political/cutting; social awkwardness -> cringe/brit; a taboo -> dark/crude), and say so if useful. When in doubt, **cutting + specific** is the least bland default.
